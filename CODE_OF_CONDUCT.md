# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Welcome questions and differing experience levels. Respect privacy and do not publish personal information or private project data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and persistent personal attacks are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to keep the project productive and safe. Decisions should be proportionate and explain the relevant behavior when possible.

Report a problem privately to the repository owner using GitHub's available reporting tools. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive allegations or personal data.
